ORACLE EXAM PRACTICE — EMPLOYEE QUICKSTART

Once your individual plug-in installation or approved ChatGPT connection is complete:

1. Start a new ChatGPT conversation and select Oracle Exam Practice.
2. Say “Open Oracle Exam Practice.”
3. Enter the exact Oracle exam name and ID code.
4. Wait while ChatGPT checks official Oracle sources and prepares 50 questions.
5. Select your answers. Use Previous, Next, or the question numbers to navigate.
6. Select Submit for grading when ready. Incomplete questions score zero.
7. Review your percentage score and the explanations and references for missed questions.
8. Choose Download results to keep a private copy, or Practice another exam.

Single-answer questions have round selection controls. Multiple-answer questions
have checkboxes and state how many answers to select. A multiple-answer question
earns credit only when the whole selected set matches the correct set.

Every correct question earns 2 points. This practice score is not Oracle's
official exam score and does not establish whether you would pass certification.

Keep your exam conversation and results private. Each attempt expires after
48 hours. The questions are AI-generated study aids and may contain mistakes;
use the linked Oracle references to check your understanding.

INSTALLATION: See INSTALL.md. This source package must be installed and connected
in a compatible ChatGPT host before it can generate questions.
